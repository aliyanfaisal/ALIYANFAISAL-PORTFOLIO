\textit{Creator \& Lead Systems Engineer} \hfill \textbf{2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Built a system that finds arithmetic and consistency errors in PDF invoices (totals, tax, duplicate charges, missing fields) and reports each with evidence: expected value, printed value and page.
    \item Designed dual-path extraction (text/OCR with Tesseract plus a vision model), cross-checked field by field; disagreements are marked ``could not verify'' instead of guessed.
    \item Kept verification deterministic: a rules engine decides and the LLM only explains findings, with provider fallback.
    \item Built an evaluation harness and published a \href{https://invoiceinspect.app/research/InvoiceInspect-Research-Report.pdf}{research report} and \href{https://invoiceinspect.app/eval-harness-reports}{eight test reports}. On a fresh set of 30 new-layout invoices (no prior tuning), the rules extractor scored 70.3\% field accuracy and the vision extractor \textbf{97.9\%}; after rules improvements, the rules extractor reached 99.9\% on all 50 invoices of that corpus (tuned on 40) and 100\% on the 10 held out (small sample).
\end{itemize}
